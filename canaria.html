Gran Canaria is the third largest of the Canary Islands, with an area of 1560.1 km² and a population of about 863,943 inhabitants (as of 2024). The capital is Las Palmas de Gran Canaria, which is located about 210 kilometers west of Morocco in the Atlantic Ocean and offers a year-round mild climate with average temperatures between 18 and 25 degrees Celsius. The north of Gran Canaria has a mild, humid climate with lush vegetation, while the south is mostly sunny and dry. These differences are caused by the highest mountains in Gran Canaria, the Moro de la Agujereada (1956 meters) and the Pico de las Nieves (1949 meters), which is often mentioned as the highest mountain in Gran Canaria.
    The most popular places on Gran Canaria
  1. To discover the plants and biodiversity with the family, the Palmitos Park is particularly suitable. There are bus lines to the park from all over the island.
  2. The dunes of Maspalomas offer an unforgettable walk, especially in the evening and morning hours, due to their golden sand dunes. For romantics, there is almost no more beautiful place on the island at sunset or sunrise.
  3. It was once a tranquil fishing village, which creates a relaxed atmosphere due to the picturesque canals, colorful flowers and charming restaurants. In the 80s and 90s, Puerto de Mogán was often referred to as Little Venice and it is precisely because of these characteristics that it is today one of the hotpots for tourist couples and those who want to be served freshly caught fish fresh from the net on their plates.
Pro tipps
  1. The natural pools created from volcanic rock and the tides of the Atlantic Ocean in the north-east of the island promise a unique bathing experience with views of the island of Tenerife.
  2. Casa de Colón is a listed building in Las Palmas de Gran Canaria. Casa de Colón wiki. It now houses a museum whose main theme is Columbus and his travels.
  3. The smallest and highest village is the municipality of Artenara at an altitude of 1270 metres
