Top 5 views you need to watch in 2027
Gran Canaria is the third largest of the Canary Islands, with an area of 1560.1 km² and a population of about 863,943 inhabitants (as of 2024). The capital is Las Palmas de Gran Canaria, which is located about 210 kilometers west of Morocco in the Atlantic Ocean and offers a year-round mild climate with average temperatures between 18 and 25 degrees Celsius. The north of Gran Canaria has a mild, humid climate with lush vegetation, while the south is mostly sunny and dry. These differences are caused by the highest mountains in Gran Canaria, the Moro de la Agujereada (1956 meters) and the Pico de las Nieves (1949 meters), which is often mentioned as the highest mountain in Gran Canaria. It's not just the parrots in Palmitos Park that attract attention with their colorful plumage. It is also very popular in the LGBT-Community and attracts around 40,000 visitors every year with the Pride parade.
The most popular places on Gran Canaria
  1. To discover the plants and biodiversity with the family, the Palmitos Park is particularly suitable. There are bus lines to the park from all over the island.
  2. The dunes of Maspalomas offer an unforgettable walk, especially in the evening and morning hours, due to their golden sand dunes. For romantics, there is almost no more beautiful place on the island at sunset or sunrise.
  3. It was once a tranquil fishing village, which creates a relaxed atmosphere due to the picturesque canals, colorful flowers and charming restaurants. In the 80s and 90s, Puerto de Mogán was often referred to as Little Venice and it is precisely because of these characteristics that it is today one of the hotpots for tourist couples and those who want to be served freshly caught fish fresh from the net on their plates.
Pro tipps
  1. The natural pools created from volcanic rock and the tides of the Atlantic Ocean in the north-east of the island promise a unique bathing experience with views of the island of Tenerife.
  2. Casa de Colón is a listed building in Las Palmas de Gran Canaria. Casa de Colón wiki. It now houses a museum whose main theme is Columbus and his travels.
  3. The smallest and highest village is the municipality of Artenara at an altitude of 1270 metres
In addition to the above-mentioned possibilities, the island also offers many activities such as a hike up to the Rouque Nublo (1813 meters), a basalt rock, which is the landmark of the island and thus offers a magnificent view as far as Tenerife, there are climbing opportunities, mountain bike tours and parachute jumps. the most well-known of all sporting possibilities, on the other hand, is surfing and windsurfing.
In the 90s, the 42-time world champion in windsurfing Björn Dunkerbeck set new standards again and again with his skills, which he continued to refine on the coasts of Gran Canaria. He runs the BDS surf school in Playa del Ingles and continues to inspire people for this sport. In general, the "Grancanrios" also convey the attitude of being the way you are, because that's how you are right. In addition to all the beautiful and interesting sides of this island island, it should also be mentioned that drinking water is only available bottled and the tap water is only suitable for brushing teeth due to the lack of water treatment , but this is really easy to handle. The bottom line is that Gran Canaria is an island that couldn't be more diverse. Whether in nature or through people, this island manages to conquer a permanent place in your heart.
