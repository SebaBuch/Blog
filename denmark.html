Discover Copenhagen beyond the postcard views. Explore colorful waterfront streets, cozy cafés, modern Nordic design and the city’s famous cycling culture. From Nyhavn to hidden local spots, Copenhagen is the perfect blend of historic charm and contemporary Scandinavian life.

Pro tipps
  1. Explore Copenhagen by bike —pedal through colorful streets, scenic waterfronts and hidden corners while seeing the city like a local.
  2. Don’t miss Copenhagen’s top attractions, from historic landmarks to fascinating museums. Check the official Copenhagen Card Attractions page to see which attractions are included and plan your sightseeing smartly.

Copenhagen is a city that makes slow travel feel effortless. From colorful harbor houses and quiet canals to cozy cafés and bike-friendly streets, the Danish capital is easy to explore without rushing from one attraction to another.

One of the best ways to experience Copenhagen is by bicycle. The city is designed around cycling, making it simple to move between historic neighborhoods, modern architecture, and waterfront areas. Along the way, you can discover small bakeries, independent shops, peaceful parks, and hidden corners that are easy to miss when following a typical tourist route.
