Highlights

From the historical culture of Japan to the beautiful beaches and volcanic landscapes of Gran Canaria
to the calm athmosphere of Denmark. Each destination offers a distinct way to explore the world.

Japan:
Culture, Architecture, Nature, Food

The land of the rising sun. Visit old cities like Kyoto, experience
the beautiful nature of Hokkaido, enjoy the tasty food and rest at
some islands with breathtaking beaches.

Gran Canaria:
Beaches, Volcanic Landspaces, Nature, Sun

The island of eternal spring. Explore the golden dunes of Maspalomas, discover
the dramatic volcanic landscapes of the interior, enjoy the mild year round
climate and relax on stunning beaches with crystal clear water

Denmark:
Calm, Design, Coastline, History

Scandinavia's relaxed atmosphere. Stroll through the colorful streets of Copenhagen,
discover charming castles and fishing villages, enjoy the vast coastline with gentle dunes
and let yourself be captivated by Scandinavian design and the "hygge" way of life.
