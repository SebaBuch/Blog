Highlights

From the historical culture of Japan to the beautiful beaches and volcanic landscapes of Gran Canaria
to the calm athmosphere of Denmark. Each destination offers a distinct way to explore the world.

Japan:
Culture, Architecture, Nature, Food

The land of the rising sun. Visit old cities like Kyoto, experience
the beautiful nature of Hokkaido, enjoy the tasty food and rest at
some islands with breathtaking beaches.

Gran Canaria:
Beaches, Volcanic Landspaces, Nature, Sun

The island of eternal spring. Explore the golden dunes of Maspalomas, discover
the dramatic volcanic landscapes of the interior, enjoy the mild year round
climate and relax on stunning beaches with crystal clear water

Denmark:
Calm, Design, Coastline, History

Scandinavia's relaxed atmosphere. Stroll through the colorful streets of Copenhagen,
discover charming castles and fishing villages, enjoy the vast coastline with gentle dunes
and let yourself be captivated by Scandinavian design and the "hygge" way of life.

My travel blog was born from a passion for discovering new places, cultures, and experiences around the world. From the vibrant streets of Tokyo and Osaka to breathtaking natural landscapes, every journey has a story worth sharing. I created this blog to capture those moments, inspire others to explore, and show what makes each destination unique. Come along on my adventures and discover the world through my eyes.

I plan my travels by researching destinations, discovering unique places, and creating a flexible itinerary that leaves room for spontaneous adventures. Along the way, I document my experiences through photos, videos, and personal stories that capture the atmosphere of each place. I focus on the moments that make each journey special, from local culture and food to unforgettable adventures. I then share these experiences on my blog to inspire others and help them plan their own travels.

Brands and companies can get in touch with me for collaborations, travel projects, content creation, or other creative opportunities. I’m always open to working with people and businesses that share my passion for travel and discovering new places. Whether it’s a partnership, destination feature, or creative project, I’m happy to explore new ideas together. And of course, Giuliano is always welcome to reach out too!

Readers can support the blog simply by following my journeys, sharing my stories, and spreading the word to others who love to travel. Every visit, comment, and interaction helps motivate me to keep exploring and creating new content. If you enjoy what I share, you can also support me by recommending the blog to friends and family. And Giuliano can support the blog by liking it — preferably for the content, not the spelling!
